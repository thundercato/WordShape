LETTERAMBLE · QUICKFIRE

Spot it. Fill it. Keep going.

Find a word through the sparkling ?, then tap a letter from your rack. Look left to right and top to bottom. Words need at least 3 letters.

HAP?Y

Every board has 15 solvable gaps and 16 letters, including the right quantities of repeated letters. Used letters fade out. There are no refills and no red herrings.

You have three passes and three hints per board. A pass brings the gap back later for fewer points. A hint sparkles around roughly 30% of your unused rack tiles, including a correct answer. It lasts until you move on.

  • 2 points First opportunity
  • 1 point After one missed opportunity
  • 0 points After two misses, or time runs out
  • 5 points Complete a crossing pair

Words have crossword boundaries. Only the whole word counts: THERE does not also score THE, HER and ERE. Background letters are separate from the word layout. Completed words get an outline so you can see exactly where they begin and end.

A crossing must make valid words both ways. Each is shown separately with a bounce and a higher magical chime. A crossing earns five points total, even after a pass; a timed-out answer always earns zero.

Wrong letters stay in your rack and move you along. They count as a missed opportunity. Used tiles cannot be selected.

Your first gap allows 30 seconds, decreasing to 15 seconds for gap 15. A returning gap keeps its original allowance. Time runs out: that gap is lost immediately. We reveal its word in a dark colour and consume the answer tile for no points. Timeouts do not spend a pass.

The final score adds a speed bonus based on your overall active playing time: one point for every 15 seconds saved against the board’s total time allowance, capped at the number of gaps you solved yourself. Reveals, these instructions and time away from the game do not count.

Unexpected dictionary words still count when they leave a solution for the remaining gaps. If a valid letter would leave another gap impossible, it stays in your rack with an explanation and no penalty.

Keyboard: A–Z to play, Space to pass. On iPhone, Safari’s Share menu → Add to Home Screen gives the board more room.
