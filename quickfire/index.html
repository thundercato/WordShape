LETTERAMBLE · QUICKFIRE

Spot it. Fill it. Keep going.

Find a word through the sparkling ?, then tap a letter from your rack. Look left to right and top to bottom. Words need at least 3 letters.

HAP?Y

Can't fill it? Hit PASS. The square will come round again. Passing only costs points when you already have a letter that works.

  • 2 points No missed opportunity
  • 1 point One missed opportunity
  • 0 points Two or more misses
  • 5 points Words in both directions

Every correct answer gives you a fresh letter, even a red one. Wrong letters stay in your rack and move you along. They count as a miss only if you had a valid answer.

Words can grow: W?LK?NG → WALK?NG → WALKING. Each letter must make a complete word. Any dictionary word counts, including surprises.

The board refreshes when no gaps can be filled from your rack. The clock counts up, so take your time or go quickfire.

10 / 15 / 20 sets the number of planted words. Extra words can happen. Keyboard: A–Z to play, Space to pass. On iPhone, use Safari’s Share menu → Add to Home Screen.
