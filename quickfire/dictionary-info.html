Dictionary & credits

This version accepts 72,825 British English word forms, from 3 to 12 letters. Twelve is the longest word that fits the board.

The vocabulary is the same SCOWL-based British English list used by the original Letteramble game, limited to this board’s word lengths. It is an open spelling dictionary, not an official Scrabble or Oxford word list. Some valid English words may be missing; some accepted words may be unfamiliar.

Boards are planted with familiar everyday words. Every answer is checked against the full bundled dictionary, including accidental words and word extensions.

Source: SCOWL en_GB-ise 2020.12.07, via wooorm/dictionaries. SCOWL / English Speller Database.
